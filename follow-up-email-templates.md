# Follow-Up Email Templates
Copy, customize the [brackets], and send. Keep every email under 150 words.

## 1. Proposal sent
Subject: Proposal attached — [Project name]

Hi [Name],

As promised, the proposal for [project] is attached.

Quick recap: [one-line outcome], delivered by [date], for [total].

Happy to walk through it on a 15-minute call — does [two time options] work?

Best,
[Your name]

## 2. Gentle nudge (3–4 days, no reply)
Subject: Quick check-in — [Project name] proposal

Hi [Name],

Just floating this to the top of your inbox. The proposal is valid until [date].

Any questions I can answer, or anything you'd like adjusted?

Best,
[Your name]

## 3. Negotiation (they push on price)
Subject: Re: [Project name] proposal

Hi [Name],

I understand budget matters. Two options:

A) Keep the full scope at [total].
B) Trim [specific deliverable] to bring it to [lower total].

What I can't do is the full scope at the lower price — it would mean cutting corners, and neither of us wants that.

Which direction works for you?

Best,
[Your name]

## 4. Accepted — kickoff
Subject: You're booked in — next steps for [Project name]

Hi [Name],

Great news — you're confirmed. Here's what happens next:

1. Sign the attached Services Agreement.
2. Pay the [50]% deposit: [payment link/details] — [$ amount].
3. I'll send the kickoff questionnaire once the deposit lands.

Kickoff call: [propose 2 times].

Looking forward to it,
[Your name]

## 5. Declined — keep the door open
Subject: Thanks for considering me

Hi [Name],

Thanks for letting me know. If anything changes — scope, timing, budget — I'm happy to revisit.

And if you know someone who needs [service], I'd appreciate the introduction.

Best of luck with the project,
[Your name]
